## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing satisfaction survey results for Executive Education programs.

The objective is to monitor learner satisfaction, assess the perceived relevance of training programs, and transform survey responses into actionable insights through interactive Power BI reporting.

The dashboards provide an overview of satisfaction levels, response rates, feedback by certification, and qualitative comments.

## Key Features

- **Satisfaction at 6 Months:** Monitoring of learner feedback six months after training, including response rates and satisfaction with professional needs, skills implementation, and training recommendations.
- **Global Satisfaction Rate:** Analysis of overall satisfaction levels and survey response distribution.
- **Satisfaction per Certification:** Comparison of satisfaction levels across targeted certifications.
- **Comments & Remarks:** Exploration of qualitative feedback and learner comments to identify improvement opportunities.
- **Survey Monitoring:** Analysis of notifications, responses, and response rates.

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Response Rate:** 19.73%
- **Number of Responses:** 894
- **Total Notifications:** 4,532
- **Satisfaction Distribution:** Very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, little satisfied, and unsatisfied.
